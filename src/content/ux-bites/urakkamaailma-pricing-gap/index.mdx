A homeowner researching a renovation wants three things: *what does this typically cost in my city*, *what's the realistic range*, and *what drives the price up or down*. I prototyped an interactive cost explorer that answers all three from the existing data — no new collection needed.

### How I got here

I walked the live site across multiple categories and cities, documenting the data structure on each card. The same schema repeats everywhere. I collected price ranges and city distributions to build a representative mock dataset, then designed and built the explorer with AI-assisted tooling in about three hours. The prototype sits *on top of* the existing page — the cards still provide detail for users who want to browse. The new layer just answers the homeowner's first question before they commit to scrolling.
