## 01 // CONTEXT

Have you ever opened your phone to check the weather and, twenty minutes later, found yourself deep in social media with no memory of the detour? That gap between what we *intend* to do and what we *actually* do is what this project addresses.

YOU (SuperEgo) is an assistant that doesn't live inside a standalone app. It operates inside the mobile operating system itself, observing passive telemetry (app switching frequency, late-night scrolling rhythms) without monitoring private content. I led the research synthesis, behavioral framing, system interaction logic, and hi-fi prototyping.

---

## 02 // PROBLEM

Most productivity tools assume users are rational planners who just need a better to-do list. However, the core friction is not the list—it is the **autopilot habit loop**.

Key behavioral pain points identified:
- **Invisible Drift:** Users drift off task without noticing the moment of transition.
- **Autopilot Habits:** By the time someone realizes they are distracted, the moment has passed.
- **Emotions Ignored:** Traditional tools ignore stress and mood states that drive habitual impulses.
- **Intention vs. Action Gap:** A persistent disconnect exists between intentional goals and immediate physical actions.

### Dual-Process Interventions
- **System 1 (Subtle Nudges):** Haptic breathing pulses during stress detection; app delay timers during deep work blocks.
- **System 2 (Reflective Moments):** Contextual prompts asking users if they wish to adjust goals or write a letter to their future self.

## 04 // IMPACT

- **OS-Level Behavioral Framework:** Prototyped an operating-system-level assistant that adapts interventions based on real-time emotional self-awareness.
- **Dual-Process Nudge System:** Successfully integrated System 1 haptic cues and System 2 reflective prompts into a cohesive mobile interaction model.
- **Privacy-Preserving Telemetry:** Proved that meaningful behavioral nudges can be delivered through rhythm tracking without monitoring user content.
