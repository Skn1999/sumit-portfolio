## 01 // CONTEXT

With incoming European Accessibility Act (EAA) mandates, regulated organizations must make their digital products accessible without slowing down software releases. The challenge was building an open-source delivery framework and facilitation model that embeds accessibility into everyday design and code workflows from day one.

As Co-founder, I worked across research, framework design, brand strategy, and team operations. Alongside developing the framework, this included market research, value proposition refinement, creating our brand voice, and serving as primary point of contact with mentors throughout the EIT Jumpstarter program.

---

## 02 // PROBLEM

Product teams often treat digital accessibility as a late-stage compliance checklist rather than a core design requirement. Fixing accessibility issues after software is already built leads to expensive refactoring, poor experiences for users with disabilities, and constant friction between compliance leads and developers.

## 03 // APPROACH

### Open-Source Delivery Primitives
Designed an open-source framework providing accessibility design primitives and structural code guidelines so teams build accessible components from the first commit.

### Co-Design Workshop Facilitation
Developed a facilitation model that brings users with disabilities directly into early product ideation, grounding decisions in real lived experience rather than abstract compliance rules.

### Pan-European Practitioner Validation
Selected from over 900 European applicants into the EIT Jumpstarter program under the New European Bauhaus category. Stress-tested the framework and service model through practitioner feedback and mentor reviews across Europe.
