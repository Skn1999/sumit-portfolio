## 1. Problem

Product teams often treat digital accessibility as a late-stage compliance checklist rather than a core design requirement. Fixing accessibility issues after software is already built leads to expensive refactoring, poor experiences for users with disabilities, and constant friction between compliance leads and developers.

## 2. Challenge

With incoming European Accessibility Act (EAA) mandates, regulated organizations must make their digital products accessible without slowing down software releases. The challenge was building an open-source delivery framework and facilitation model that embeds accessibility into everyday design and code workflows from day one.

---

## 3. My Role

As Co-founder, I worked across research, framework design, brand strategy, and team operations:

- **Framework & Facilitation:** Co-designed the open-source delivery framework and workshop model to help teams turn accessibility guidelines into practical sprint tasks.
- **Research & Mapping:** Conducted qualitative research with accessibility experts and end-users with disabilities to translate WCAG and EAA requirements into clear design tokens and developer guides.
- **Brand & Value Proposition:** Defined Groundwork's strategic positioning, brand voice, and value proposition.
- **Accelerator Operations:** Managed team milestones, internal communications, and mentor engagements throughout the 8-month EIT Jumpstarter program, pitching through multiple evaluation rounds to the Grand Final.

---

## 4. Solution & Key Work

### 01 // Open-Source Delivery Framework
Designed an open-source framework providing accessibility design primitives and structural code guidelines so teams build accessible components from the first commit.

### 02 // Co-Design Workshop Model
Developed a facilitation model that brings users with disabilities directly into early product ideation, grounding decisions in real lived experience rather than abstract rules.

### 03 // Pan-European Validation
Selected from over 900 European applicants into the EIT Jumpstarter program under the New European Bauhaus category. Stress-tested the framework and service model through practitioner feedback and mentor reviews across Europe.

- **2nd Place Grand Finalist (out of 900+ European applicants):** Reached the Grand Final of the EIT Jumpstarter 2025 program in the New European Bauhaus category.
