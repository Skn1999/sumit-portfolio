# Task 022: Homepage Copy Optimization — Human-in-the-Loop AI & Product Oversight Positioning

**Task Name:** Homepage Copy Optimization — Human-in-the-Loop AI & Product Oversight Positioning

**Context for the Task:**
Recruiters and product leaders today face a major dilemma: AI tools generate UI mocks and frontend code at unprecedented speeds, but when AI operates unsupervised, it causes costly production mistakes, hallucinates workflow patterns, and creates generic, soulless user experiences. 

Sumit brings a unique dual superpower to solve this:
1. **4+ years of Enterprise Frontend Engineering** (production-grade code, state architecture, scalability).
2. **Double Master's in HCI** from Aalto University & University of Trento (cognitive ergonomics, user research, human-centered design principles).

The objective of this task is to rewrite the homepage copy so Sumit stands out to recruiters as the ultimate **Human-in-the-Loop Product Designer & AI Experience Architect**—the leader who oversees and orchestrates AI design & development processes to guarantee speed without risking user trust, code quality, or costly product failures.
