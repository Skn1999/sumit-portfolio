## The Vertical Scale Challenge

### Claustrophobic Depth Without Pagination
In the Apple TV series *Silo*, ten thousand people inhabit a 144-story subterranean bunker where elevators are forbidden, forcing residents to traverse massive spiral stairs. Traditional promotional web experiences break scale by splitting narrative across pagination or carousels.

To convey this claustrophobic scale on the web:
- The entire 144-floor structure needed to exist as a single, uninterrupted vertical ribbon.
- Scrubbing the scroll wheel had to feel like descending physically down the central shaft.
- Every major level (Level 01 Work, Level 48 Process, Level 96 Mechanical & Farm, Level 144 The Depths) needed immediate inspection without interrupting momentum.

> **The Structural Constraint:** Delivering a continuous 144-floor descent without stalling mobile devices required avoiding heavy 3D mesh geometry in favor of hardware-accelerated 2D canvas particles and synchronized CSS matrix transforms.

---

## Dual-Layer Camera Choreography

The visual engine synchronizes two parallel representations of the silo: an architectural technical line drawing and localized high-resolution photographic plates.

### Technical Mechanics

1. **Continuous Vertical Ribbon (`trackYAt`)**: As scroll progress advances from 0 to 1, the world coordinate system smoothly pans vertically from the upper surface rim to the submerged flooded foundations.
2. **Dynamic Zoom Dip**: During travel segments between floors, the virtual camera dips slightly outward before zooming tightly in on arrival, mimicking an intentional physical camera move.
3. **Double-Buffer Crossfades**: At each level dwell, the technical drawing smoothly crossfades into the photographic plate, revealing the gritty reality of life on that floor.

---

## Spatial Intelligence: 70vw VisionOS Glass HUD

At each level dwell, the interface materializes a centered glass panel inspired by Apple VisionOS spatial design guidelines:

- **70% Viewport Width**: Expands across the stage to provide comfortable reading room for 3-column sector data, cast roles, and narrative context.
- **Multilayer Backdrop Blur**: Employs frosted glass blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`) with subtle hairline specular borders to separate typography from the textured background.
- **Direct Mode Toggling**: Visitors can toggle into "Inspect Silo" ghost mode, fading the UI panel to 8% opacity to examine the underlying architectural details.

---

## Canvas Particles & Dynamic Atmosphere

To make the subterranean environment feel physical and living:
- **Upward Dust Motes**: A 2D canvas streams 60 particles drifting gently upward, with velocity responsive to scroll acceleration.
- **Atmospheric Color Grading**: CSS overlay mixes dynamic gradient washes, transitioning from cold clinical steel at Level 01 down to deep mechanical amber at Level 144.
