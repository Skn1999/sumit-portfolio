## 01 // CONTEXT

Gosta Labs develops an AI-powered clinical documentation assistant that transcribes and summarizes doctor-patient conversations in real time to reduce physician paperwork burden. After securing 11 healthcare customers and growing revenue in Finland, the startup needed an international expansion strategy and an internal operational upgrade to support scaling beyond Finland.

Executed as part of the *Global Business in the Digital Age* program (Aalto University & Gosta Labs), I led the **Internal Development & Operational Readiness** track: assessing organizational readiness, conducting qualitative interviews with the CEO, authoring a 3-year development roadmap, and co-presenting final recommendations to the executive board.

---

## 02 // PROBLEM

Expanding into Central and Eastern European (CEE) markets (Poland, Czechia, and Romania) presented two main friction areas:

- **External Market Barriers:** Navigating different national healthcare regulations, local language models, hospital procurement systems, and varying levels of Electronic Health Record (EHR) maturity across target countries.
- **Internal Operational Gaps:** Gosta Labs lacked formalized role definitions, competence mappings, standardized sales-to-product feedback loops, and a structured resource allocation model needed to scale internationally.

---

## 03 // APPROACH

### CEE Go-to-Market Strategy
Analyzed market entry opportunities across Poland, Czechia, and Romania. Evaluated local hospital procurement workflows, competitor landscapes, EHR integration paths, and flexible pricing models for healthcare providers.

### Competency & Hiring Plan
Built a 12–24 month competency mapping and hiring plan to address talent gaps, define role clarity, and establish clear internal mobility paths for upcoming regional teams.

### Operational Process Formalization
Documented core internal workflows and established feedback loops between sales and product development teams to ensure customer insights directly informed product iterations.

### Scalable Resource Allocation
Designed a quarterly resource planning model to align workload distribution, team hiring, and budget allocation with GTM rollout milestones.

---

## 04 // IMPACT

- **Executive Strategy Handover:** Delivered a dual-track strategy combining a CEE market entry plan with a 3-year internal development roadmap directly to Gosta Labs' CEO and executive board.
- **Operational Expansion Blueprint:** Provided a structured hiring, process, and resourcing roadmap to support international expansion without disrupting existing Finnish operations.
