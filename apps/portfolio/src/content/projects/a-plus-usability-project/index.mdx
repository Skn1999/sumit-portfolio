## 01 // CONTEXT

A+ is an open-source learning platform developed and used at Aalto University for course delivery, assignments, and student interaction, primarily in computer science. While its technical capabilities are strong, its usability limits were known, yet nobody had an evidence-based picture of how they affected real students—especially users with accessibility needs.

Executed as part of the *Collaborative Evaluation of Interactive Systems* course at Aalto University, our group of 4 evaluated A+'s usability and accessibility through a dual-lens methodology: combining heuristic inspection with user testing sessions involving participants with disabilities.

---

## 02 // PROBLEM

A+ had known usability friction points, but the core engineering team lacked structured evidence to prioritize what to fix:

- **Navigation Inefficiencies:** Complex course structures made it tedious for students to track progress and navigate long assignment pages.
- **Accessibility Barriers:** Inadequate focus indicators, low text contrast, and poor keyboard trap management created roadblocks for screen-reader and assistive technology users.
- **Subtle Compounding Issues:** Rather than single catastrophic bugs, multiple minor interface inconsistencies compounded into high cognitive load for all learners.

---

## 03 // APPROACH

### Inspection-Based Evaluation
Identified usability issues, assessed the platform against Nielsen's heuristics, and benchmarked compliance against WCAG 2.1 AA standards. Rated each finding by severity.

### User-Based Testing with Disabled Participants
Conducted direct observation and contextual interviews with participants with disabilities to validate our inspection findings and discover unpredicted real-world obstacles.

### Affinity Mapping & Synthesis
Compared severity ratings between the two evaluation lenses. Synthesized findings into 5 core interaction themes: Navigation Efficiency, Accessibility & Readability, Interface Design, User Experience, and Submission Feedback.

### Co-Design & Developer Prioritization Workshop
Facilitated a structured workshop with problem cards to agree on high-leverage fixes and ideate feasible design solutions with stakeholders.

---

## 04 // IMPACT & RECOMMENDATIONS

Delivered an actionable developer roadmap to the A+ development team, including:

1. **Semantic Color Palette:** A compliant token system establishing clear contrast hierarchies across UI states.
2. **Accessible Navigation:** High-contrast focus states, sticky navigation sidebars, and accessible progress landmarks.
3. **Optimized Feedback Loops:** Clearer assignment submission status notifications and inline error validation.
