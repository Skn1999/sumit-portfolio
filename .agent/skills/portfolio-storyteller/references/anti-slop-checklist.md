# Anti-AI-Slop Writing Checklist & Editorial Rubric

This reference defines the strict editorial standards required for all portfolio case studies. Any draft generated by this skill must pass this audit with zero violations.

---

## 1. Banned Vocabulary & Buzzwords

Never use the following generic AI superlatives, empty transitional fluff, or corporate jargon:

| Category | Banned Terms (Do Not Use) | Acceptable Senior Alternatives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Empty Superlatives** | *seamlessly, seamless, revolutionary, innovative, cutting-edge, unparalleled, bespoke, holistic, robust, meticulously* | Specify the mechanism: *deterministic, zero-latency, type-safe, validated via 12-user audit, 200ms debounce* |
| **AI Cliché Verbs** | *delve, delve into, foster, elevate, empower, embark, illuminate, harness, orchestrate, navigate, encapsulate* | Use direct active verbs: *built, measured, cut, audited, resolved, shipped, structured, separated, mapped* |
| **Fluff Transitions** | *a testament to, at the heart of, plays a crucial role, paving the way for, in today's fast-paced world, it is important to note* | State the constraint or outcome directly without throat-clearing sentences. |
| **Vague UX Jargon** | *intuitive, user-friendly, frictionless, visually appealing, delightful, empathy-driven* | Name the measurable interaction: *glanceable in <5s, 1-click evidence drawer, 4.2/5 SUS score, zero nested menus* |

---

## 2. Banned Syntactic Patterns & AI Tells

1. **The "By [gerund], [subject] [verb]" Formulaic Sentence**:
   - ❌ *By leveraging an innovative design system, the team was able to elevate the user experience.*
   - ✅ *We extracted 14 shared tokens into Tailwind, cutting UI state regressions across the 3 core views.*

2. **The "Not only X, but also Y" Structure**:
   - ❌ *This not only streamlined the workflow, but also empowered users to act with confidence.*
   - ✅ *Surfacing telemetry spikes directly cut mean-time-to-intervention from 14 minutes to 90 seconds.*

3. **Passive Textbook Double Diamond Recitation**:
   - ❌ *First we conducted 5 interviews, then created an empathy map, then developed personas, then drafted wireframes...*
   - ✅ *Initial occupant interviews revealed a contradiction: users opened windows for ventilation but forgot to close them once temperature dropped below 18°C. This dictated our 5-state ambient recovery loop.*

4. **The "Tapestry / Symphony / Beacon" Metaphors**:
   - Immediate rejection if words like *tapestry, beacon, symphony, interplay, cornerstone* appear.

---

## 3. The 4 Senior Craft Mandates

Every section written must adhere to these four tenets:

### Tenet 1: Constraint-First Framing
Never describe a feature in isolation. Always ground it in the structural constraint or human friction that forced its creation.
* Example: *"Classroom teachers cannot remain logged into a web dashboard while managing 25 students. Ambient in-room hardware was mandatory."*

### Tenet 2: The Google XYZ Formula
Format accomplishments as:
> **Accomplished [X]**, as measured by **[Y]**, by doing **[Z]**.
* Example: *"Reduced triage verification time from 4 minutes to <30 seconds (100% source verifiability) by replacing multi-paragraph summaries with an instant side-drawer evidence inspector."*

### Tenet 3: Decisive First-Person Voice
Speak with authentic agency. Say *"I prioritized X over Y because..."* or *"I disconfirmed my initial hypothesis when..."* rather than anonymous corporate passive voice (*"A decision was made to..."*).

### Tenet 4: Mandatory Authentic Compromise
Real senior work is defined by trade-offs. Every case study MUST explicitly detail at least one real compromise in the retrospective (e.g., dropped scope, engineering constraint, lack of legal compliance APIs, or time-boxed design token handoff).

---

## 4. Pre-Publish Verification Audit

Before presenting the draft to the user, run this verification:

- [ ] Zero occurrences of banned words (*seamlessly, delve, testament, holistic, foster, robust, intuitive*).
- [ ] No generic textbook process narration without project-specific tension.
- [ ] At least one disconfirmed hypothesis or unexpected research finding included.
- [ ] At least one concrete technical/scope compromise clearly documented.
- [ ] All metrics are grounded in actual numbers or validated qualitative feedback quotes.
- [ ] Tone is calm, direct, and human-scale (Linear/Vercel/Rachel Chen aesthetic).
